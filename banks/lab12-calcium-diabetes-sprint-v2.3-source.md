### Question 1

Where is nearly all calcium in the adult body stored?

A. In plasma bound to albumin  
B. Inside skeletal muscle fibers  
C. In the kidneys as filtered calcium  
D. In bone as hydroxyapatite crystals  

**Correct answer: D. In bone as hydroxyapatite crystals**

**Explanation:** About 99% of body calcium is stored in bone as hydroxyapatite; only a small fraction is in blood and cells.
