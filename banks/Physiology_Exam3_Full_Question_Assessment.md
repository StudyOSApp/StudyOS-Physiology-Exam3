## Bloom Classification Method

- Existing explicit Bloom metadata was preserved and normalized to Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, or Create.
- Items without Bloom metadata were classified from the actual task in the stem—not merely from difficulty labels.
- Foundational identification or direct retrieval was classified as **Remember**.
- Mechanism, interpretation, and conceptual explanation were classified as **Understand**.
- Scenarios, predictions, treatment effects, and pathway consequences were classified as **Apply**.
- Multi-variable patterns, localization, feedback puzzles, and clinical discrimination were classified as **Analyze**.
- No item was assigned Evaluate or Create unless the learner actually had to judge competing evidence or produce a new response; multiple-choice format alone does not justify those levels.

## Assessment Notes

- The engine now contains four distinct pools: Learn, Practice, Labs, and Lab Practical.
- The new practical bank is intentionally concentrated at Apply and Analyze because it emphasizes pathway reasoning, stimulation/suppression, pattern interpretation, and clinical localization.
- Bloom assignments marked **Inferred** are an assessment of the current learner-facing item. They should be treated as audit classifications rather than source-author claims.
- This report assesses inventory, organization, and cognitive demand. It is not a scientific-content or distractor-quality audit of every individual question.
