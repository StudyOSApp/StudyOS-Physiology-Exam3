# Non-Lab Answer-Length Audit

## Scope

- Original Practice questions: 479
- Newer Learn and Practice questions: 608
- Total non-lab questions reviewed: 1,087

## Findings

The extreme-outlier audit identified 40 questions in which the keyed answer could be inferred from its visibly greater length:

- Original Practice bank: 15
- Newer Learn and Practice banks: 25

## Refinement

- Concisely rewrote the keyed answer when the same physiology could be stated naturally in fewer words.
- Rebalanced multiple distractors when shortening the keyed answer alone would have produced awkward wording.
- Preserved every correct-answer letter, question stem, question ID, explanation, difficulty level, question type, and metadata record.
- Synchronized edited standalone banks with their available JSON or Markdown source files.

## Validation

- Questions reviewed after refinement: 1,087
- Correct-answer letter changes: 0
- Question-stem changes: 0
- Remaining extreme keyed-answer length outliers: 0

The extreme-outlier rule flagged a keyed choice when it was the longest by at least 20 characters and at least 50% longer than the distractor average, or when it was at least twice the distractor average and at least 40 characters long.
