### Question 1

Which anterior pituitary cell type produces growth hormone?

A. Corticotropes  
B. Thyrotropes  
C. Somatotropes  
D. Lactotropes

**Correct answer: C. Somatotropes**

**Explanation:** Somatotropes are the anterior pituitary cells that synthesize and secrete GH under the balance of hypothalamic GHRH and somatostatin.

Which hypothalamic hormone stimulates release of TSH?

A. TRH  
B. CRH

Which mechanism most accurately describes normal control of prolactin secretion?

A. Direct stimulation by a hypothalamic releasing hormone  
B. Negative feedback from circulating milk proteins  
C. Cortisol-mediated inhibition

### Question 34

In severe hemorrhage, ADH can rise sharply before osmolarity has meaningfully changed because:

A. Osmoreceptors are triggered directly by blood loss  
B. A large baroreceptor signal can override the osmotic set-point
