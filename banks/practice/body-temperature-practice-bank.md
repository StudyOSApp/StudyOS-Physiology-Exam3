### Question 1

Why does core temperature vary less than skin temperature during ordinary environmental changes?

A. Core temperature is tightly regulated  
B. Skin contains no blood vessels  
C. Core tissues cannot produce heat

**Explanation:** Feedback mechanisms stabilize core temperature while skin exchanges heat with the environment.
