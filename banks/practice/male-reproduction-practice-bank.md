### Question 55

Because testosterone is lipid soluble, it acts on target cells through:

A. Secretory vesicles that release cGMP  
B. Extracellular calcium-based receptors  
C. Intracellular receptors that regulate transcription  
D. Cell-surface sodium channels  

**Correct answer: C. Intracellular receptors that regulate transcription**

**Explanation:** Testosterone crosses the membrane and binds an intracellular androgen receptor.
