### Question 1

Where is most of the body's calcium stored?

A. Bone  
B. Plasma  
C. Skeletal muscle  
D. Extracellular fluid

**Correct answer: A. Bone**

**Explanation:** About 99% of body calcium is stored in bone, leaving only small fractions in cells and extracellular fluid.

### Question 50

Why can fat malabsorption contribute to osteomalacia?

A. It increases renal PTH destruction  
B. It reduces absorption of fat-soluble vitamin D  
C. It causes excessive calcitonin release  
D. It converts osteoblasts into osteoclasts

**Correct answer: B. It reduces absorption of fat-soluble vitamin D**

**Explanation:** Poor fat absorption can reduce vitamin D uptake and calcium availability, impairing adult bone mineralization.
