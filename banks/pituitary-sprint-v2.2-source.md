### Question 1

Why is the hypothalamus described as a master gland in this lecture?

A. It produces every circulating hormone

A. Nipple suckling to sensory nerves to PVN activation to oxytocin release to myoepithelial contraction  
B. Nipple suckling to PVN activation to myoepithelial contraction to oxytocin release to sensory nerves  
C. Nipple suckling to sensory nerves to dopamine release to lactotroph inhibition to milk ejection  
D. Nipple suckling to osmoreceptors to ADH release to renal water retention to milk ejection

**Correct answer: A. Nipple suckling to sensory nerves to PVN activation to oxytocin release to myoepithelial contraction**

**Explanation:** Suckling sends sensory input to the PVN, leading to oxytocin release and contraction of mammary myoepithelial cells that ejects milk.
